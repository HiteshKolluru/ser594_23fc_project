Description:   
The project aims to analyze popular songs to uncover the factors that contribute to their success and the sentiments they convey. It will explore the most popular genres, examine word usage and trends, and analyze the characteristics that make certain artists stand out, including the types of songs they create.

Intellectual Merit: This project contributes to the understanding of contemporary music culture by systematically analyzing popular songs and their lyrics. By employing text analysis techniques, it uncovers the underlying themes, sentiments, and linguistic trends that characterize chart-topping music. This research will not only highlight the factors that drive a song's popularity but also offers insights into genre preferences and artist success, fostering a deeper appreciation for the intersection of language and music in shaping societal trends.

Data Sourcing: using the billboards website to get list of popular songs(I will scrape the website), using Lyricsgenius to get lyrics of the songs for each of the songs I get to make a file that will have song, artist, lyrics and other relevant information.

Research Objective:

Objective 1: Identify Popular Genres: Analyze the distribution of genres within the top 100 hits to determine which genres dominate the charts and explore how genre influences lyrical themes and success.

Objective 2: To predict the sentiment label (positive, negative, neutral) of lyrics from the top 100 hits based on their word usage and emotional tone.

Objective 3: Classify songs based on the words in the lyrics.

Objective 4: To evaluate the relationships between lyrical characteristics (e.g., genre, sentiment, and word frequency) and the popularity of songs in the top 100 hits.
